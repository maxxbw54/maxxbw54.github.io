Advice on Writing a Paper

  1. Use human language to write the abstract and intro section. People are losing patience due to too many AI-generated papers. Cutting the chase at the beginning is a good start.
  2. Make sure you describe the problem clearly.
  3. You should be the lead of the paper writing, not AI. You should be the one in charge of the main flow of the writing.
  4. Focus more on whether your research questions are good enough (with clear evidence/support on why answering this question is important).
  5. None of the experiments is perfect, do NOT overrely on AI. AI can always find something to criticize in your work.
  6. Remember the audience of your paper is human (mainly, at least for now), so whatever strategies make writing human reader-friendly still applies.
  7. Paper review is very random, focus on yourself and the work itself. Don't be bothered too much about the decision.

Writing a paper is just one part of the research journey, not even the most important part. But try to enjoy writing (if u do not), because you're training yourself in story telling (in an exciting way).

Good luck! 🤞
